About Us

Who we are and what we stand for.

We provide legal solutions to clients in all sectors of commerce and industry, government, resource owners and individuals.

KESNO LAWYERS is a nationally owned law practice operating from Lae, Morobe Province. Established in 2016, the firm provides legal services to clients throughout Papua New Guinea.

Our approach is multidisciplinary. Alongside the practice of law, we are developing our work in management, governance and business where these needs are intertwined with the legal and professional needs of our clients.
